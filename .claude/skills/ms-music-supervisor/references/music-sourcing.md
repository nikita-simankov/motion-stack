# Music Sourcing

Library search, brief derivation, selection criteria.

## Deriving the musical brief

From brief.json:

- Q3 runtime → tempo range
- Q5 tone → mood, energy
- Q6 concept posture → risk appetite for the score
- Q7 visual direction → texture of the score
- Q12 music posture → source path
- Q14 deadline → sourcing vs composing
- Q16 budget tier → library tier

From concept.json:

- structural family → energy arc

From boards.json:

- transition frames → hit opportunities
- CTA frame → out cue target

## Library tiers

Tier 1 (Premium, cleared for every platform):
Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, Soundstripe, Premium Beat

Tier 2 (Standard, cleared for most platforms):
YouTube Audio Library, Bensound, Uppbeat, Pixabay Music

Tier 3 (Free, limited):
Free Music Archive, Incompetech, ccMixter

Rules:

- Every library must offer:
  - Commercial use
  - Monetization on the target platforms
  - Content ID clearance
  - Territory worldwide
- Libraries without Content ID clearance are rejected for YouTube distribution.
- Libraries with restricted territories are rejected when the audience is global.

## Selection criteria

When sourcing, rank candidates by:

1. Mood match to brief.Q5 and the musical brief
2. Tempo within the derived range
3. Energy arc shape match to the concept's structural family
4. Duration sufficient (equal to or longer than the runtime)
5. License cleanliness (all flags green)
6. Preview listen — the supervisor listens before selecting

Rules:

- No track is selected on metadata alone.
- No track is selected if its energy arc fights the concept's arc.
- No track is selected if it has vocals that would fight the VO.
- Instrumental-only by default.
- Vocals allowed only when the brief's tone and the concept permit.

## Composing

When the posture is original or the budget tier is premium:

- Commission or compose a cue to picture
- Deliver as a stem with separated elements: drums, bass, melody, pads
- Deliver aligned to the concept's energy arc
- Deliver with a cue sheet

Composed cues carry the studio as the publisher, or the commissioned composer per the agreement.

## Client-supplied

When the posture is client-provided:

- Client supplies the track file
- Client supplies the license reference
- Studio verifies the license covers every platform in brief.Q4
- Studio does not clear the track
- Studio ducks and mixes

## Rejected tracks

- Tracks with an unclear license
- Tracks that expire inside the publication window
- Tracks that are not cleared for monetization when the video is monetized
- Tracks that are not Content ID cleared for YouTube
- Tracks with a territory excluding the audience
- Tracks with vocals fighting the VO
- Tracks with an energy arc that fights the concept

## Sourcing workflow

1. Derive the musical brief.
2. Search the library tiers in order.
3. Shortlist 3-5 candidates per cue type.
4. Listen to each.
5. Rank and select.
6. Record the license at selection time, not later.
7. Cut to picture.

## Preview and approval

The music stem preview is the artifact the client approves at gate 11. Not the track, not the cue sheet — the mixed stem. The cue sheet is emitted alongside for legal review.

## Cost

- Library subscriptions are per-account, not per-track. Record the tier and the per-track cost if applicable.
- Composed cues are per-cue, budget from Q16.
- Client-supplied cues cost nothing to the studio.
- Cost is recorded in the run report.
