# Platform Rules

Per-platform rules: aspect, duration, codec, copy, hashtags.

## LinkedIn

Aspect: 1:1, 4:5, or 16:9
Resolution: 1080x1080 / 1080x1350 / 1920x1080
Duration: 90s feed max, 10m native max
Codec: h264 main 4.2
Bitrate: 8-14 Mbps
Loudness: -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP
Captions: Burned + SRT
Thumbnail: 1200x1200 (1:1) or 1280x720 (16:9) or 1080x1350 (4:5)

Copy:

- First 210 characters matter for feed
- No emoji unless the brand voice permits
- No hashtag in the first line
- 3-5 hashtags at the bottom
- One CTA

Hook culture:

- Professional claim or specific number
- Slower read than vertical
- No slang

Banned:

- Engagement bait ("comment YES")
- Sensational claims without a source
- Excessive hashtags (LinkedIn penalizes)

## Instagram Reels

Aspect: 9:16
Resolution: 1080x1920
Duration: 90s max
Codec: h264 main 4.2
Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
Loudness: -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP
Captions: Burned + SRT
Thumbnail: 1080x1920 cover frame

Copy:

- First 125 characters matter (before "more")
- Emoji allowed when the brand voice permits
- Hashtags in the first comment, not in the body
- 5-10 hashtags total
- One CTA

Hook culture:

- Visual moment or surprising claim
- Fast
- Frame-one text high and central

Banned:

- Reposted content without transformation
- Watermarked content from other platforms
- Banned hashtags (check the current list)

## TikTok

Aspect: 9:16
Resolution: 1080x1920
Duration: 60s optimal, 10m max
Codec: h264 main 4.2
Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
Loudness: -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP
Captions: Burned + SRT
Thumbnail: 1080x1920 cover frame

Copy:

- First 100 characters matter
- Native, conversational tone
- 3-5 hashtags
- One CTA

Hook culture:

- First 0.5 seconds matter
- Frame-one text and frame-one motion
- First line of caption is a native-sounding provocation

Banned:

- Watermarks from other platforms
- Engagement bait
- Misleading claims

## YouTube Shorts

Aspect: 9:16
Resolution: 1080x1920
Duration: 60s
Codec: h264 main 4.2
Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
Loudness: -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP
Captions: Burned + SRT + VTT
Thumbnail: 1280x720 (search display) or 1080x1920 (in-stream cover)

Copy:

- Title: max 100 chars, first 40 visible
- Description: max 5000 chars, first 150 visible
- Tags: max 500 chars total
- Hashtags: up to 3 in the title area, up to 15 total
- One CTA (subscribe or comment)

Hook culture:

- Closer to TikTok but slightly more explanatory
- Frame-one text and motion
- First line of caption reads as a question or claim

Banned:

- Clickbait titles
- Misleading thumbnails
- Content that violates YouTube's community guidelines

## X (Twitter)

Aspect: 16:9 or 1:1
Resolution: 1920x1080 or 1080x1080
Duration: 140s max
Codec: h264 main 4.2
Bitrate: 8-14 Mbps
Loudness: -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP
Captions: SRT (burned captions optional, preferred off for X)

Copy:

- First post carries the hook
- Thread if the video needs more than 280 chars of context
- 1-2 hashtags max
- One CTA

Hook culture:

- Text-dominant
- Video autoplays muted; on-screen text must read at 25% scale

Banned:

- Engagement farming
- Misleading claims
- Excessive hashtags

## Cross-platform rules

- No platform gets the same first line as another.
- No platform gets the same hashtag set as another.
- No platform gets the same CTA string as another.
- No banned hashtags.
- No watermarks from a different platform.
- No content that violates the platform's community guidelines.
- No claims that the run's dossier does not support.

## Duration caps

If the master exceeds a platform's duration cap:

- Re-cut from the master's beat map to fit the cap.
- Do not speed up the video.
- Do not truncate the CTA.
- Do not drop the hook.
- Preserve the concept's structural family.
- Prefer cutting a middle beat over cutting the hook or the CTA.

## Aspect mapping

- 9:16 → Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- 1:1 → LinkedIn feed, Instagram feed, X (option)
- 4:5 → LinkedIn feed, Instagram feed
- 16:9 → LinkedIn native, X, YouTube standard

Rules:

- A platform that accepts multiple aspects gets the one closest to the master.
- If the master is 9:16, LinkedIn gets 1:1 or 4:5, X gets 1:1.
- If the master is 16:9, vertical platforms get a 9:16 re-cut.
- If the master is 1:1, LinkedIn gets 1:1, vertical platforms get a 9:16 re-cut, X gets 1:1.
