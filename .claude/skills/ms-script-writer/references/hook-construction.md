# Hook Construction

The first 2 seconds (frames 0-60 at 30fps) decide the video. If the hook fails, nothing after it matters.

## The three hooks

Every hook lands three things simultaneously, inside the first 60 frames:

- Visual hook — one image or one motion. Not a logo. Not a title card. Not a montage.
- Text hook — <= 8 words on screen. Concrete. The claim, the number, or the provocation.
- Audio hook — the first VO syllable, the first SFX hit, or a deliberate silence that reads as intent.

If any of the three is missing, the hook is not built.

## Accepted patterns

### Specific number

Visual: a single large number animating up.
Text: "4.2 billion calls. One quarter."
Audio: VO states the number.
Why it works: specificity earns attention.

### Contradiction

Visual: two states side by side, one clearly winning.
Text: "The faster path is the slower one."
Audio: VO states the contradiction.
Why it works: the audience resists, then leans in.

### Named subject

Visual: a recognizable product, protocol, or person, minimally framed.
Text: "MCP broke our pipeline. Here's why we kept it."
Audio: VO names the subject.
Why it works: the audience self-selects in one second.

### Stakes first

Visual: the failure state, not the success state.
Text: "Your agent forgot the last three calls."
Audio: VO states the failure.
Why it works: pain is more specific than promise.

### Demonstration in progress

Visual: the thing working, mid-action, no setup.
Text: "Watch this."
Audio: SFX from the action.
Why it works: the audience watches to see the outcome.

## Rejected starts

Never open with:

- "In this video" or "In today's video"
- "Today we're going to"
- "Have you ever wondered"
- "Let's dive in"
- "Hi, I'm [name]"
- A logo animation longer than 15 frames
- A title card
- A rhetorical question unless the concept is question-first
- A wide establishing shot with no subject
- A montage of B-roll with no anchor
- A quote from a famous person
- A statistic with no context ("Studies show...")

## Rewrite rules

- If the hook would work for a competitor's video on the same topic, rewrite it.
- If the hook is a question, it must be the exact question the audience is already asking.
- If the hook is a number, the number must be specific and cited.
- If the hook is a contradiction, the contradiction must be earned by the body.
- If the hook is a demonstration, the visible action must complete within the runtime.

## Testing the hook

Ask three questions:

1. Would this stop the scroll on the target platform? (Yes / No)
2. Does it state or imply a claim from the dossier? (Yes / No)
3. Could it be reused for a different video on the same topic without change? (No = good)

Any "No" on 1 or 2, or "Yes" on 3, is a rewrite.
