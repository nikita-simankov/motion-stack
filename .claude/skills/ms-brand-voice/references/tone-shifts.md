# Tone Shifts

Tone shift rules per platform and per context.

## Per platform

### LinkedIn

Register: professional, measured, specific.
Energy: low-medium.
Emoji: not allowed unless the brand voice explicitly permits.
Slang: not allowed.
Hashtags: at the bottom, 3-5, no hashtag in the first line.
Length: first 210 characters matter; body 3-6 sentences.

### Instagram Reels

Register: conversational, energetic, hook-forward.
Energy: medium-high.
Emoji: allowed when the brand voice permits.
Slang: light, brand-appropriate.
Hashtags: in the first comment, 5-10.
Length: first 125 characters matter; body 2-4 sentences.

### TikTok

Register: native, casual, first-person.
Energy: high.
Emoji: allowed.
Slang: allowed, brand-appropriate.
Hashtags: 3-5 at the end of the caption.
Length: first 100 characters matter; body 1-3 sentences.

### YouTube Shorts

Register: explanatory, question-forward.
Energy: medium.
Emoji: sparing.
Slang: light.
Hashtags: up to 3 in the title area, up to 15 total in the description.
Length: title first 40 chars matter; description first 150 chars matter.

### X

Register: compressed, declarative, thread-native.
Energy: medium.
Emoji: sparing.
Slang: light.
Hashtags: 1-2 in the first post.
Length: 280 chars per post; thread if longer.

## Per context

### Hook

Energy: highest.
Register: the platform's register.
Length: shortest.
Rule: one claim or one question, no preamble.

### Proof

Energy: lowest.
Specificity: highest.
Register: the platform's register, minus any flourish.
Rule: numbers, sources, specifics.

### Objection

Register: calm, direct.
Rule: acknowledge, answer, move on. No defensiveness.

### CTA

Register: clear, single action.
Rule: one action, no alternatives, no hedging.

## Cross-platform consistency

Voice is constant. Tone shifts. The attributes do not change between platforms.

Rules:

- LinkedIn and TikTok share the same attributes.
- LinkedIn and TikTok do not share the same register.
- A line spoken on LinkedIn is adapted to TikTok, not copied.
- The platform's tone overrides the default tone when they conflict, except for banned terms and attributes.

## Recording

Every tone shift check records the platform, the artifact, and pass/fail.

An artifact fails if it violates the platform's register, energy, emoji rule, slang rule, or length rule.

## Rejected patterns

- Same first line on LinkedIn and TikTok
- Emoji on LinkedIn when the brand voice does not permit
- Slang on LinkedIn
- Corporate register on TikTok
- Hashtag spam on any platform
- Any platform's tone applied to a different platform's artifact
