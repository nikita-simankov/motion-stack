# Claim Verification

## Confidence scoring

High — one tier-1 source, or two independent tier-2 sources, or one tier-1 plus one tier-2.
Medium — one tier-2 source, or two tier-3 sources with independent methods, or one tier-1 with an acknowledged caveat.
Low — one tier-3 source, or any claim with an unresolved contradiction.

Used claims must be high or medium. Low claims go to the unused section.

## Aging

A claim has a shelf life if it describes a market, a price, a headcount, a version, or a state that changes. Record the expiresIn window based on the topic's rate of change:

- Fast (AI, crypto, social platforms): 30-90 days
- Medium (SaaS, hardware, market share): 90-180 days
- Slow (standards, fundamental research, history): 1 year+

If a claim expires before the video's deadline, flag it and either replace it or note it as time-bound in the script.

## Contradictions

When two sources disagree:

1. Compare tiers. Higher tier wins unless the lower tier is more recent and the topic is fast-moving.
2. Compare dates. More recent wins on fast-moving topics.
3. Compare scope. A narrower claim does not contradict a broader one; reconcile by scope.
4. If still unresolved, record the contradiction, downgrade the claim to medium or low, and flag it to the writer.

Never silently pick a side.

## Independence

Two sources from the same publisher, author, or research group are one source. Two sources reporting the same press release are one source. Independence requires different methodology, different data, different ownership.

## Recency priority

On fast-moving topics, recency beats tier. A tier-3 blog with today's numbers may beat a tier-1 paper from 2018. Record both, use the recent one, cite both.

## Verification checklist

For every used claim:

- [ ] Source accessible at the recorded URL, or archived
- [ ] Source date recorded
- [ ] Scope preserved from source to claim
- [ ] Confidence word preserved
- [ ] Caveats preserved
- [ ] At least one independent corroboration if confidence is medium
- [ ] Contradictions recorded
- [ ] Aging window recorded
- [ ] Not sourced solely through an aggregator
